Collected with care

More than a folder of images.

A library that follows your edits

Edit artwork in Finder or Explorer. Added, renamed and removed images are reflected in Coverly. Right-click a preview to reveal its current file.

The story behind the soundtrack

Review composers, film years, singers and track lists. Bring reference pages into the album, then choose the details you trust.

Better covers, ready for your music

Compare similar artwork, review restoration results and export square JPEGs under 500 KB for song metadata.

Keep the archive exactly where you want it.
Choose a folder outside the app. Readable directories use Album (1982), Album (YYYY) for an unknown film year, and _1, _2 for name conflicts. Updating the app leaves that archive in place. File indexing changes database records and preview caches while preserving your artwork.

A simple start

Install. Choose a folder. Make it yours.

01 / INSTALL

Open your native package

Choose the right Mac DMG, Windows Setup or complete Portable ZIP. The app includes its Python runtime.

02 / CONNECT

Choose your archive

Select an existing folder containing coverly.sqlite3, or create a new archive. Your files stay separate from the application.

03 / COLLECT

Bring in your artwork

Import sleeves and disc scans, or discover permitted website catalogues. Use Coverly's local interface at localhost:8000.
